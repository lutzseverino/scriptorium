`OUT-OF-SCOPE.md` and `agents/openai.yaml` are unchanged from that commit. `SKILL.md` keeps upstream's roles, invocation, discovery buckets, outcomes, and templates, except that `needs-info` means missing information that the orchestrating agent answers or routes, and `ready-for-human` means work that needs the owner's own accounts or credentials, or a taste call the owner reserved. It replaces the single triage procedure with routing by uncertainty: research and independent acceptance for code-bound requests, and for intent-bound ones fact-finding, inference the orchestrating agent can cite, and a grilling session with the owner that drafts the briefs. It routes every question through the orchestrating agent, with its decision inbox as the one channel to the owner. It also points the label mapping at the installed triage label configuration instead of the setup skill, phrases the disclaimer as a method step, adds who does what, notes that a quick state override is the maintainer's own review, and has acceptance choose between `ready-for-agent`, `ready-for-agent` with the factory's skip label, and `ready-for-human`. `AGENT-BRIEF.md` keeps upstream's principles, template, and examples, and adds the decisions principle and the triage notes. Upstream changes reach this fork only when Repo Canon ports them.

`ready.mjs` is Repo Canon's original readiness acceptance command. The acceptance step calls it to apply the reviewed revision and confirm the validator recorded it.
