# Credits

This skill is Repo Canon's own, taken from the `triage` skill in [Matt Pocock's skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills). Its upstream base is tag `v1.3.1`, commit `24fe0ef7737efae15c87225755e9f6f5965e4888`, at [`skills/engineering/triage`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/24fe0ef7737efae15c87225755e9f6f5965e4888/skills/engineering/triage). That material is used under its MIT License, whose notice follows at the end of this file; adoption also installs it beside the skills. The skill differs from its upstream base: `SKILL.md` states that a prompt or brief naming the skill is its invocation. It adds a Reads section, which lists the repository files the skill reads and what it does when each is missing. `OUT-OF-SCOPE.md` and `agents/openai.yaml` are unchanged from it. `SKILL.md` keeps upstream's invocation, discovery buckets, outcomes, and templates, and names its roles by Repo Canon's label families: `type:` categories and five `state:` roles naming whom an issue waits on, with `wontfix` as the closing outcome. Upstream's `needs-info` becomes `state:reporter`, kept for a fact only an outside reporter holds, beside `state:grill` and `state:owner` for the owner's intent and answers; `ready-for-human` becomes `state:owner`, which is not readiness; and triage sets a `priority:` label by the installed rules' rubric without replacing a person's. It replaces the single triage procedure with routing by uncertainty: research and independent acceptance for code-bound requests, and for intent-bound ones fact-finding, inference the orchestrating agent can cite, and a grilling session with the owner that publishes direct specification/ticket contracts for independent review. It routes every question through the orchestrating agent, with its decision inbox as the one channel to the owner. It also points the label mapping and meanings at the installed triage label configuration instead of the setup skill, replaces the disclaimer with the installed comment rules' section that each posting step names, adds who does what, notes that a quick state override is the maintainer's own review applied through the readiness command, and has acceptance choose between `state:ready`, `state:ready` with the factory's skip label, and `state:owner`. The Reads section also covers `ready.mjs` and the installed comment rules. `AGENT-BRIEF.md` keeps upstream's principles, template, and examples, and adds the decisions, progress and live-acceptance principles and the triage notes. Its reference condenses durability and behavioral guidance, repeated template placeholders, and bug, enhancement, PR and negative examples while retaining the original seven fields, every example contract, available-input and decision provenance, independent acceptance, owner-review designation and revision/readiness semantics. `ready.mjs` is Repo Canon's original readiness acceptance command. The acceptance step calls it to apply the reviewed revision and confirm the validator recorded it. The research handoff identifies the issue comments as the assigned research artifacts and destinations, composing with the research skill's direct investigation and assigned publication method. The code-bound brief followed by triage notes and intent-bound fact-finding notes remain distinct deliverables. A second mode runs the skill unattended as the factory's triage run: it researches, writes and reviews the brief itself, cites only repository records for inferred intent, writes nothing on the tracker, and ends with a run result that asks the factory to post the brief or question and apply its labels. The skill points to the contribution guide and installed rules for the triage policy instead of restating it. Its method consolidates mode and role summaries, folds resumption into context gathering, and states the reporter template and Reads fallbacks compactly while preserving the research, independent acceptance, readiness and factory-result procedures. It distinguishes prepared comment publication from explicit body-mode publication, preserves one verbatim Original request and the unchanged inventoried legacy exception, keeps host attestation separate from mutable reports, and checks v2 platform-observation freshness through the validator. The template, examples and independent review add Seams after Out of scope, citing codebase-design’s single Ownership check definition. The writer records its result and independent review assesses honesty, Value owner, consumers, reversibility and cost, while the validator checks structure. The readiness command also checks body-observation freshness for v3 Seams exemption results. Its intent-bound grill publishes one specification and proposed direct tickets through to-spec/to-tickets, instead of draft Agent Briefs for the orchestrator to file. Independent acceptance checks the split and seam/factual evidence without an owner quiz, preserves original intake publication rules and applies direct-contract shapes without redundant briefs or category labels. It handles amendment revisions and exact native-link failures. Model suggestions, acceptance and the factory triage result cite the installed Model selection rule and record its guidance-based reason, bounded by supplied host capabilities.
